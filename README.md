# Mental Footprint Reader — SE-1-BW-CTPF

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache_2.0-blue.svg)

## 🇮🇩 Indonesia

Baca jejak mental dari tulisan.

Framework ini membantu psikolog dan masyarakat umum untuk mengamati pola pikir, emosi, dan karakter seseorang dari teks pendek (1-2 kalimat hingga paragraf), berdasarkan kernel pemikiran Sky Elon (Barry Wuwungan) sejak 2025.

### Prinsip Framework

| Prinsip | Penjelasan |
|---------|------------|
| ✅ Deskriptif, bukan diagnostik | Memberi petunjuk kuat, bukan vonis atau diagnosis klinis |
| ✅ Butuh data cukup | Minimal 5 teks, 500 kata, dari 2 konteks berbeda |
| ✅ Etis | Ada batasan dan disclaimer di setiap output |

### 5 Indikator Jejak Mental

| Indikator | Yang Diamati |
|-----------|--------------|
| Ritme | Panjang-pendek kalimat, jeda, repetisi |
| Tekanan Emosi | Tanda seru, kapitalisasi, kata seru |
| Pilihan Kata | Diksi konkret/abstrak, kata kerja aktif/pasif |
| Konsistensi | Keselarasan antara klaim dan nada |
| Framing | Takut, Tenang, atau Agresif |

### Cara Penggunaan

1. **Untuk menganalisa orang lain:** kumpulkan minimal 5 teks dari berbagai situasi (netral, emosional, interaksional)
2. **Untuk refleksi diri:** cukup 1 teks, minta AI membantu Anda melihat pola Anda sendiri
3. **Jika data kurang:** AI akan menjawab "maaf, data masih kurang"

### Batasan Framework

> ⚠️ **Framework ini BUKAN alat diagnosis. Jangan digunakan untuk:**
> - Mendiagnosis gangguan mental
> - Keputusan hukum atau diskriminasi
> - Menyerang atau menyudutkan orang lain

## 🇬🇧 English

Read mental footprints from text.
This framework helps psychologists and the general public observe thinking patterns, emotions, and character from short texts (1-2 sentences to paragraphs), based on Sky Elon (Barry Wuwungan)'s 2025 thought kernel.

### Framework Principles

| Principle | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| ✅ Descriptive, not diagnostic | Provides strong clues, not verdicts or clinical diagnoses |
| ✅ Requires sufficient data | Minimum 5 texts, 500 words, from 2 different contexts |
| ✅ Ethical | Built-in limits and disclaimers on every output |

### 5 Mental Footprint Indicators

| Indicator | What to Observe |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Rhythm | Sentence length, pauses, repetition |
| Emotional Pressure | Exclamation marks, capitalization, interjections |
| Word Choice | Concrete/abstract diction, active/passive verbs |
| Consistency | Alignment between claim and tone |
| Framing | Fearful, Calm, or Aggressive |

### How to Use

1. **To analyze others:** collect at least 5 texts from different contexts (neutral, emotional, interactional)
2. **For self-reflection:** just 1 text, ask AI to help you see your own patterns
3. **If data is insufficient:** AI will respond "sorry, data is still insufficient"

### Framework Limitations

> ⚠️ **This framework is NOT a diagnostic tool. Do not use for:**
> - Diagnosing mental disorders
> - Legal decisions or discrimination
> - Attacking or cornering others
